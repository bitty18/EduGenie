8. Project Demonstration --- EduGenie

1. Demonstration Objective

Demonstrate the working EduGenie prototype and show the four
student-learning workflows.

2. Application Flow

The student: 1. Opens EduGenie in a browser. 2. Enters information into
one of the feature sections. 3. Clicks the relevant button. 4. Receives
a formatted result or an input warning.

3. Demo 1 --- Ask EduGenie

**Input:** A student question.

**Action:** Click `Ask EduGenie`.

**Current prototype output:** A success message and a demonstration
answer about Java inheritance.

4. Demo 2 --- Topic Explainer

**Input:** A topic name.

**Action:** Click `Explain Topic`.

**Current prototype output:** A success message and a demonstration
explanation in student-friendly language.

5. Demo 3 --- Notes Summarizer

**Input:** Pasted notes.

**Action:** Click `Summarize Notes`.

**Current prototype output:** A success message and demonstration
summary text.

6. Demo 4 --- Practice Question Generator

**Input:** A topic name.

**Action:** Click `Generate Questions`.

**Current prototype output:** Three generic practice questions.

7. Empty-Input Demonstration

* For each feature, clicking the button without entering the required
input displays a warning message.

Examples: - "Please enter a question." - "Please enter a topic." -
"Please paste your notes."
